Welcome to my page, here you'll find information about my professional life and research projects.

I am a lifelong outdoors enthusiast passionate about fishing, geology, hydrogeologic interactions, and sustainability. Formally trained in marine geology, geophysics, and geospatial technologies I strive to integrate modern scientific techniques with research to create practical solutions for protecting coastal environments.
